From the blog

How our network is built to stay online

NEXUS runs on a star topology: every computer connects directly to a central managed switch, which links to our router and wireless access points over Cat6 Ethernet cable.

  • Easy to manage and monitor from one point
  • Faster to troubleshoot when something goes wrong
  • If one PC or cable fails, every other station stays connected
  • A layout well suited to busy cyber café environments

  • RouterInternet gateway
